# Analysis Engine

All calculations are deterministic and versioned.

## Current result

Below, within, above, unavailable, qualitative, or ambiguous according to the interval supplied by that report.

## Reference position

For finite lower and upper bounds: lower, middle, or upper third.

## Previous comparison

Use only the immediately preceding comparable value. Show absolute delta, meaningful percentage delta, elapsed time, status transition, changed-interval warning, and comparability notes.

## Tendencies

Short term: risen, fallen, stable, no comparison.

Long term, requiring at least three comparable points: repeatedly rising, repeatedly falling, broadly stable, variable, insufficient data.

No prediction or extrapolation.

## Attention ordering

Explainable aggregation of visible changes only. It is not a risk score or disease probability.
