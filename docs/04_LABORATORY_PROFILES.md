# Laboratory Profiles

Profiles are organizational views, not diagnoses.

Initial set:

- complete blood count;
- iron metabolism;
- inflammation;
- renal function;
- liver and biliary system;
- thyroid;
- glucose metabolism;
- lipid metabolism;
- electrolytes and minerals;
- coagulation;
- bone metabolism;
- cardiac markers.

A parameter may belong to multiple profiles. Missing profile members are shown only as not present, never required or recommended. Initial definitions are demonstration data and are not clinically validated.
