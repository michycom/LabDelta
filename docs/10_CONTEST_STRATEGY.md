# Contest Strategy

## Pitch

LabDelta is a privacy-first desktop tool, intended to be released as open source, that helps clinicians see important changes hidden across longitudinal laboratory reports without generating diagnoses or recommendations.

## Demo

Global priority dashboard; expanded severe patient; current/prior/long-term comparison; overlapping profiles; source traceability; safe patient-bound import; local deterministic architecture; staged Codex development and validation.

Do not claim proven diagnostic improvement or lives saved.
